ChoreDash · mobile redesign

Cozy Cream

Matched to the soft vibe of your current app — cream ground, sage + rose accents, rounded cards with colored spines, circular icon chips, gentle shadows. Turn 3a, revised: the app's real bottom bar (Tasks · Chores · add in the middle · Memos · Settings), in-list search and sort, a chore pressure view (days since / days until / overdue), a monotone Zen list — everything at once, no pressure colors — and the one-at-a-time screen recast as the reminder view. Serif page headers. Turn 4a adds the deeper screens behind the tabs — edit-task & chore-detail sheets, appearance/themes, display & smart-visibility steppers, reminders & alerts, and about. Turn 5a is the Zen Dark theme plus a card revision that supersedes 3a: tighter rows, no progress bars, one Lucide icon per category, NFC scan in the header, a bottom bar you can actually read. Turn 7a now ships in both dark and light. Turn 8a is the final direction — the decisions locked across every turn, in one place.

8A Final direction what ships, in one place — resolves the two themes carried since 5a and gives 7a's edit sheets a light counterpart
Locked decisions
Theme: Zen Dark (5a) ships as default; Cozy Cream is the light alternate — both carry the same tokens, so switching is a palette swap, not a rebuild. Edit sheets (7a) now exist in both.
Cards: 5a's compact revision — no progress bars, colored spine + one Lucide icon per category, cadence line ("every 28d · done 7w ago"). 3a's original cards are historical only.
Navigation: real bottom bar — Tasks · Chores · + (speed dial: new memo/task/chore) · Memos · Settings.
Edit & log sheets: one grammar for chores and tasks — title-as-input, grouped compact settings card, pickers right-aligned, destructive action demoted below the fold (6a, 7a).
Model: NFC chore logging, Fresh/Aging/Stale status, task priority + due, A/M assignees — unchanged from the real app, per github.md.
